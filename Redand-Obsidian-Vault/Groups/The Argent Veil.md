The Argent Veil is an ancient anti-magic guard. The organisation was used by all the kingdoms in Northen Redand post the Tyranny of the Mages.

They have a symbol of a **silver veil draped over a black sun**, representing the suppression of magical light.

![[TheArgentVeilpng.png|177]]

The Argent Veil exists to **control, suppress, and regulate magic** in all its forms. They believe unchecked magic is a threat to mortal civilization, claiming that arcane power corrupts and divine magic manipulates free will. *"Order through Purity."*

## Philosophy & Doctrine:
- **Magic is a disease**—a temptation that leads to chaos and tyranny.
- **Mortal men were never meant to wield the power of the arcane.**
- **Purity of flesh and mind** is the highest virtue; magic users are considered tainted.
- They justify their actions as **protection against magical tyranny**.

## Purity Through Control
The Argent Veil still believes magic is dangerous, but they’ve adopted a pragmatic stance:  
*"To cleanse the world of corruption, we must wield the blade of corruption itself."*

**Magic is a weapon**, and like any weapon, it can be turned against its own kind. Their justification:
- **Controlled magic is a necessary evil** to prevent greater catastrophes and Tyranny.
- **Only the sanctified may wield it**—those who undergo rigorous trials and binding oaths.
- **Magic is a poison; the Veil is the antidote.**
## Structure
- **High Inquisitor** – Supreme leader, often a charismatic zealot with no magical ability.
- **Silver Tribunal** – A council of judges who interpret doctrine and issue decrees.
- **Purifiers** – Elite agents trained to hunt magic users; often equipped with anti-magic gear.
- **Veilbinder** – These are usually former mages who have sworn loyalty and undergone magical branding to ensure obedience.
- **Scriveners** – Scholars who catalog magical artifacts before destroying or sealing them.
- **Penitents** – Former magic users who have renounced their power and now serve as informants or spies.
## Methods & Tools:
- **Anti-Magic Shackles** – Suppress spellcasting when worn.
- **Null Rods** – Portable zones of anti-magic.
- **Sanctified Brands** – Marks burned into the flesh of repentant spellcasters.
- **Truthfire Trials** – Interrogations using flames that react to magical essence.
## How They Use Magic
- **Sanctioned Spellcasters**: Known as **Veilbinders** (described above).
- **Bound Magic**: Spells are cast through relics inscribed with nullifying runes, limiting their scope and preventing wild surges.
- **Counter-Arcanum**: Specialized magic designed to unravel other spells—dispels, wards, and anti-summoning rituals.
## Headquarters:
**The Argent Bastion** – A fortress built on a leyline nexus, inverted with runes to disrupt magical flow. Its walls are lined with silver and cold iron, and the air hums with nullifying energy.
It is located in [[Ragloedan]].
