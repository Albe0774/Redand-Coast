At the top of the Hierarchy in the Church of [[Abreona]] sits The Abreonan high counsel. There are a total of 5 members of the High Counsel.

The most important role of the high counsel is to choose members of the assembly and bishops.

The high Counsel sits for life. When one dies or abdicates the assembly have to find one among themselves to take their place. This usually takes a long time.

While this goes on the assembly can only enact emergency laws. The rest of the high Counsel decide if a law is an emergency law. This is only if there is 3 or more council members. If not then a person gains a temporary seat on the high counsel. The identity of this person is written in letters written by the dead counsel member. The letter is written when they gain their seat in the council. So while the counsel is fully seated there is always 5 secret letters with the identity of the temporary replacements in the high counsil should 2 or more die or abdicate.
