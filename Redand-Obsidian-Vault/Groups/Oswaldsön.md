Oswaldsön is an old noble familiy with connections to the [[Arrowhost]] area of [[Edrea]].

They have a vineyard a weeks travel south east of [[Arrowhost]]. Their primary export is Wine. [[The Elegant Timberland]] is the only place in [[Edrea]] hot enough to grow grapes, thus have The Oswaldsöns taking up the trade.

Mambers of the Family include:

- **Erik Oswaldsön** – Patriarch of the house, stern and principled. Known for his tactical mind and strong leadership.
  
- **Ingrid Oswaldsön** – Erik’s wife, originally from Riverrest. Graceful and intelligent, she manages the estate’s affairs with quiet authority.
  
- **[[Brandur Oswaldsön]]** – Firstborn son and heir. Trained in diplomacy and swordsmanship, he is being groomed to take over the estate.

- **Bodil Oswaldsön** – Daughter of Erik and Ingrid. Known for her sharp wit and interest in herbalism and lore.

- **[[Sigmund Oswaldsön]]** – Youngest son. Adventurous and rebellious, often found wandering the woods.
  
- **Nanna Oswald** – Ingrid’s illegitimate daughter from Riverrest. Formally adopted by Erik, she lives on the estate and is close to her half-siblings.

### Influence
**Oswaldsön Estate**: A fortified manor surrounded by fertile farmland and ancient woods. Known for its vineyards, horse breeding, and a small chapel dedicted to [[Idis, God of Emotion]].

 **Riverrest Ties**: Through Ingrid, the family maintains quiet connections to Riverrest, a town known for its artisans and river trade.
 
 **Political Standing**: Loyal to [[Fallenclaw|The Fallenclaws]]. The Oswaldsöns prefer diplomacy over confrontation but are not afraid to defend their honor.

### Lore and Reputation

House Oswaldsön is often seen as the “old guard” of nobility—traditional, honorable, and somewhat aloof.
  
Rumors swirl about Nanna’s true parentage, though none dare speak openly.

The family is known for hosting seasonal festivals at their estate, drawing nobles and commoners alike.
