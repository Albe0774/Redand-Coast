## Structure

The council consists of representatives of each of the five families.
- The [[Fallenclaw]]s who control the lumber surrounding [[Arrowhost]]
- The [[Evenrock]]s control the quarry's in the eastern part of [[The Colourless Hills|The Hills]]
- The [[Abafort]]s control [[The Gap of Light|The Gap]] and with it the iron mines of [[Ironmere]] and also the acces to [[The Frozen Highlands]]
- The [[Polanne]]s who feeds most of [[Edrea]] with the big expanse of farmland of the [[Maymark]]
- The [[Machetré]] sits on the biggest mercantile company in [[Windcall]] and therefore have more wealth then any other in [[Edrea]]

There is no single monarch. Instead, the council elects a **High Chair** every five years, a position that grants limited executive authority but immense symbolic power. Decisions require a majority vote.

## Responsibilities

Roads, bridges, and public works are funded collectively but managed by individual families. Each family maintains its own forces, which can be called upon for national defense. There is no standing royal army—only the combined strength of the council. Trade agreements, tariffs, and resource allocation are debated fiercely, as each family seeks to protect its own interests.

### Power dynamics

Families are expected to contribute their resources for the good of [[Edrea]]. Resources are often traded privately between families in exchange for favors, influence, or political leverage. This barter system keeps the council in a delicate equilibrium—until someone tries to tip the scales.
