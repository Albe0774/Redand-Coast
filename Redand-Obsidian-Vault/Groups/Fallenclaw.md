Fallenclaw is a family ruling over the area surrounding [[Arrowhost]]. They are one of the families in ruling in [[The Council of Edrea]].

At the head of the Family sits [[Ivar Fallenclaw]]. Even though he in principle have absolut power he listens to the council of his advisors.

At his right hand sits [[Gunhild Fallenclaw|Gunhild]]. She acts as a master of coin and is responsible for both Fallanclaw and [[Arrowhost]] funds.

Gunvor is educated from [[Snowmire]] and is an expert in foreign relations and diplomacy. She has diplomats stationed both in [[Snowmire]] and [[Windcall]] ensuring Fallenclaw interest.

Ove is in charge of Internal Affairs. He has spies all over town and in all the villages sourunding [[Arrowhost]].

Brenna and Ingmarr command the Arrowhost and Fallenclaw militaries. This includes the city watch and other units stationed in the surrounding area. 

Jorunn is a monk of The Order of The Wise Hand. She has acces to the large library and archives in the Cathedral in [[Arrowhost]].

Asger and Frode is the voices of the Church at the Table. Asger as part of The Eternel Cycle, and Frode in The Golden Flame.
