Kateus is one of two gods officially worshipped in [[Edrea]]. Where the other would be [[Idis, God of Emotion|Idis]].

Kateus usually deals with any questions regarding logic and reasoning.

The Order of Wise Hand worship Kateus as the Aspect of Knowledge.
The Order of The Eternal Cycle also worship Kateus, but as the Aspect of Order. 
## Divine Domains
### Aspect of Creation
Domains include: 
**Arcana**, **Forge** & **Knowledge**
### Aspect of Order
Domains include: 
**Nature**, **Order** & **Twilight**
### Aspect of Rest
Domains include: 
**Peace** & **Twilight**
