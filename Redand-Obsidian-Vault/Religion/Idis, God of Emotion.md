Idis is one of two gods officially worshipped in [[Edrea]]. Where the other would be [[Kateus, God of Reasoning|Kateus]].

Idis usually deals with any questions regarding emotions.
## Divine Domains
### Aspect of the End
Domains include: 
**Death** & **Grave**
### Aspect of Renewel
Domains include: 
**Life** & **Light**
### Aspect of Conflict
Domains include: 
**Tempest**, **Trickery** & **War**
