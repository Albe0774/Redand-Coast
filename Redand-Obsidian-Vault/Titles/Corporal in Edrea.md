A title one can hold in any military in [[Edrea]]

### Qualifications
One must have finished their training as a privat in the Army.

For one to be considered for the rank of Corporal, one must have shown great courage or extraordinary valor. The rank is reserved for those Privates that stand out.

### Duties
With the rank comes a duty to be an example for their fellow soldiers. They are usually second (or first if there isnt a sergent) in command in a group. They are to carry out their CO's orders.

### Benefits
The rank of Corporal comes with a salary of 4Sp pr. day and also food and a place to rest. The salary is paid after agrement with the Corporals CO, however this is usually done biweekly.
