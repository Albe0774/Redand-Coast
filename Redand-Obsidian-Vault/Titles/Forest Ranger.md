Forest Ranger is a Company/Rank in [[Arrowhost|Arrowhosts]] military.

They report to no one, however they have to answer a colonel or in [[Arrowhost]] if they do their job poorly.

### Qualifications
When a captain receives an order from their CO in [[Arrowhost]] for the recruitment of a new Ranger, they have the authority to choose who they see fit.

### Appointment
As stated earlier, a colonel has to order the recruitment of a Ranger. However the Captain of a battalion is usually the one to request a new recruit, since they are the one who sees the need for one.

### Duties
It is a Rangers duty to take care of the Citys interests woods of the timberland.

It is the responsibility of the Rangers to make sure the city can run their operation in the woods. This means protecting workers, escorting caravans, reconing unexplored territory and other such tasks.

### Benefits
Anyone accepting the title forego their weekly salary. However they are now to be compensated like any other hired mercenary, by the authorities. And they still have a right to a place to sleep and a meal at any military outpost in [[The Elegant Timberland]] region.
