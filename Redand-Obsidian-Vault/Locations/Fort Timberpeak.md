A fort laying 22 miles northwest of [[Arrowhost]]. 
Points of interest in the fort include [[Pinwood Inn]], The Smithy and the market.
### Quartermaster
In the fort there is a quartermaster named Morgran. Hes a dwarf. Hes usually traning recruits how to keep their arrows sharp, rather then giving equipment for soldiers. That job belongs to Magnhild. A tall, broad-shouldered woman.

Members of the army can buy equipment taxfree. Recruits a also giftet a free Chain shirt, Knife and Spear (they have to make their own bow). Every soldier in the army can also get 10 arrows or bolts a week.
