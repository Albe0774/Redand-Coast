Arrowhost is a City in the eastern part of the Country [[Edrea]], called [[The Elegant Timberland]]. The city is ruled by [[Fallenclaw|The Fallenclaws]].

![[Arrowhost.jpg|700]]

## Districts
The city of Arrowhost is divided into six distinct districts:
- **The Workers’ Quarters**
- **The Carpenters’ District**
- **The Industrial District**
- **The Barracks**
- **The Western district**
- **The Upper Residence**

At the Upper Residence, a private army funded by the Fallenclaw family patrols the grounds. As a result, anyone not explicitly welcome is swiftly escorted out.

# Worldbuilding

## Points of Interest
Around the town square, you'll find a **Temple**. To the northeast lies the **Market Square**, where locals trade farm produce and small game.

In the western part of town lies a high end inn called [[The Owl Nest]]. A cozy inn notable for its stuffed Owlbear. Used by hunters as a temporary sanctuary.

At the top of the hill, beside the Fallenclaw residence, stands the city’s grand **Library**—a towering structure of stone and lore. Though gifted to the city by [[Fallenclaw|The Fallenclaws]], the noble family still retains ownership of the vast archive hidden beneath its foundations.

## Demographics
Most of the population of Arrowhost is made up of workers, employed either in the mills or in the forestry industry. There are also carpenters working in the inner city, who are of a higher social status than the ordinary laborers. Then there are soldiers, living modest but proud lives, respected by the middle and upper classes. Lastly, there is the upper class, primarily members of the Fallenclaws, but also ambassadors from other families and nobles.

The most common non-human race in Arrowhost is the elf. This is due to the proximity to [[The Elegant Timberland]], which is the last friendly place for elves in Edrea. Xenophobia against elves is prevalent, as many of the wild elves living in the Timberland have made life difficult for the workers in the woods. Consequently, elves are now often blamed for violence everywhere.
## Goverment
[[Fallenclaw|The Fallenclaws]] own most of the industry and land in and around Arrowhost. Therefore, they have enough wealth to pay for soldiers; alas, they also rule the town.

Most workers are not paid much in coin but instead receive a place to live and just enough food for themselves and their families. However, during times of scarcity, their food supplies are the first to be rationed.

Anyone making money in the city must pay 40% of their income in taxes, including the Fallenclaws. These taxes go to the city and are used to fund the city guard and infrastructure. However, it is the Fallenclaws who manage the administration of these funds, and the accounting records are not open to the public.

There is also a 25% sales tax. Registered companies can be exempt from paying this tax on official business expenses.

Furthermore, any farms growing grain or other foodstuffs must pay a tithe—again, to support the soldiers—and this too is administered by the Fallenclaws.
## Defenses
The city's military force is dominated by skilled archers, as the abundant local wood makes arrow production both cheap and efficient. As a result, the bow has become the favored weapon among the populace. Less experienced guards, however, are typically issued crossbows for their ease of use and reliability.

Atop a steep hill stands the Upper Residence—a formidable monument that commands the surrounding landscape. Once a monastery, it now serves as the home of  [[Fallenclaw|The Fallenclaw]] family. Its heavy fortifications render it nearly impregnable, making it both a symbol of power and a bastion of defense.

To the east of the district lie the barracks, teeming with guards and designed for rapid deployment.

Encircling the entire city is a towering stone wall, reinforced with guards stationed at every gate. These sentinels keep watch day and night, ensuring that no threat breaches the city's defenses.
## Industry & Trade
Most of [[Arrowhost|Arrowhost's]] industry revolves around woodworking in one form or another.

Many laborers work in the industrial district, tending to forges, warehouses, and the bustling lumbermills. Others venture into the surrounding woods, where they either reside or travel each morning to fell timber. Some spend their days navigating the river, ferrying logs downstream to the city.

Local estates also offer a modest number of farming jobs—for example, the [[Gramish]] farms just outside the city. However, the majority of the grain consumed in [[Arrowhost]] is imported from the fertile Maymark region. Wild game is also hunted freely in the nearby forests, providing an additional source of food and trade.
## Infrastructure
Toward the lake lies a large industrial port and a modest shipyard. The district is packed with warehouses and factories, while watermills hum along the rivers that run through [[Arrowhost]].

On the hillside leading up to the Upper Residence, a small park overlooks the entire city, offering a rare moment of peace amidst the bustle.

At the summit, the grand residential complex of the Fallenclaws rises like a fortress reaching for the sky. Surrounding it are a handful of the city's most luxurious homes. Behind the residence stands a vast library and archive, gifted to the city by the Fallenclaws—though they still retain ownership of the sprawling archive complex hidden beneath the library.

[[Arrowhost]] lacks a formal sewer system. Instead, sewage from the eastern part of town is collected and sold to the [[Gramish]] farms surrounding the city. And there is local sewers under the [[Fallenclaw]] residence.

Residents of the eastern district typically rely on the nearby river for water, which, fortunately, is never far away.
## Assets
A single blacksmith operates within the Barracks, crafting weapons and armor for the city’s defenders. The forge is known as **The Elegant Anvil**, a place where steel meets artistry.

In the Carpenters’ District, a jeweler specializes in delicate wooden jewelry—small, intricate pieces carved with care and often adorned with local motifs.

On the western edge of town, a modest mercantile shop welcomes travelers, buying and selling whatever goods they bring from afar. Its inventory is ever-changing, shaped by the flow of wanderers and merchants.

Aside from these, the city’s small market offers only farm produce and wild game, harvested from the surrounding lands and forests.

## Guilds and Factions
The carpenters of Arrowhost are proud—and rightly so. Their craftsmanship is renowned across Edrea, producing the most exquisite ornaments and finely crafted furniture in the realm.

Naturally, [[Fallenclaw|The Fallenclaw]] family maintains a powerful presence throughout the city, their influence felt in every district.

The officials won’t speak of it openly, but in the eastern part of town, the workers quietly gather at an local inn called [[The Rusty Raven]]. It’s said they hold sway over much of that area, organizing and watching over their own with a quiet but firm authority.
## Architecture
Most of Arrowhost is built almost entirely from wood and logs, giving the city a rustic and cohesive charm. A few buildings are half-timbered, showcasing more refined craftsmanship. On the western side of town, however, bricked structures with clay-tiled roofs stand out, hinting at wealth or older construction.

In the center of the town square sits a beautiful, small **Temple**, a place of quiet reverence and community gatherings.

Atop the Upper Residence, the awe-inspiring **Library** towers over the city—a beacon of knowledge and legacy, visible from nearly every district.
