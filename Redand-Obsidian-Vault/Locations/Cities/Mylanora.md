Mylanora is a forest clearing acting as a gathering place for elves living in the southern part of [[The Elegant Timberland]]. This is in the western most part of [[Edrea]].

The elvish word for a forest clearing is called a lant. A lant is a kind of city however the elves don't really live in cities but more spread out. 

In Mylanora there is 6 primary points of interest:
- The Open Plaza
- The Barracks
- The Visiting Huts
- Marketplace
- The Book Shelter
- The Residency
### The Open Plaza
Most notably there is a plaza formed like a full amphitheater where any questions needing the community’s judgement will be heard.

An elder listens to anyone who wants to voice their opinion and then passes judgement. Even though they have the final say, they need to listen to the people otherwise they are quickly replaced by someone else who the people have more confidence in.

Only elves, and those the elves find worthy, are allowed to participate in the meetings. When a meeting is taking place the plaza is surround by members of [[The Protectors of the Forest]].

The plaza is in the northernmost end of the clearing, just on the coast of a medium size lake.

### The Barracks
To the west of the plaza, there is a place for warriors to train, rest and sharpen their weapons.

At the front, a firepit can be seen always lit. Volunteers from [[The Protectors of the Forest]] can often be seen making stew, eating, sharpening their swords and socialising.

Behind the firepit there is a target range for archers to practice.

To the south of the archery range and firepit, there is a small smithy open for use. Anyone who wishes to use the smithy are welcome. A small donation box hangs at the edge of the area. It is common courtesy to donate when using the smithy to support its upkeep. [[The Protectors of the Forest]] are responsible for the upkeep but are always looking for a helping hand.

### The Visiting Huts
At the southern end of the clearing is a path leading into the forest due east. Here there are small huts where people who aren't permanent residents can live temporarily while they are visiting the clearing, whether it's for business or pleasure.

Elves don’t need to sleep, but there are a few huts with gurney style beds for medical purposes.

### Marketplace
In the middle of the clearing, what was once a temporary marketplace, has now been made permanent. Here traders from different tribes come to sell and buy goods they are missing. Many tribes rely on the trade provided for survival. Most smaller necessities can be traded here.

### The Book Shelter
To the right of the plaza a permanent book shelter has been set up to shelter books and scrolls.

The purpose of the shelter is to store scrolls and books from the weather.

Forretningen er fyldt med bøger og scrolls ude foran, på to etager. Bagved kan anes et mere lukket område. Scrolls med spellsene **Fog Cloud** og **Jump** kan findes, de koster 10 Gp hver.

A public shelf or single space can be rented for cheap, however anyone can read these. A private space can be rented for 5 times the price. If items are left after payment is up, the Shelter will claim the item and try to sell them. If the original owners come back for the item, they must purchase the items back. Afterwards they can either pay for renewal of the storage space or reclaim the items.

Prices for renting a storage space:

| Size of Space              | 1 day  | 1 week | 1 month | 1 year |
| -------------------------- | ------ | ------ | ------- | ------ |
| A single Item              | 5 Pp   | 3 Cp   | 2 Sp    | 15 Sp  |
| A shelf (10 books)         | 25 Pp  | 15 Cp  | 10 Sp   | 75 Sp  |
| A private single item      | 25 Pp  | 15 Cp  | 10 Sp   | 75 Sp  |
| A private shelf (10 books) | 125 Pp | 75 Cp  | 50 Sp   | 375 Sp |
Besides books and scrolls, writing supplies can be bought here: ink, quills, and parchment.

Her arbejder en gnom kvindelig bibliotekar som hedder Menny og en Lore keeper elver der hedder Norro.

Norro er en ældre elver. Selvom det ikke ses på ham. Han holder sig som regel i baglokalet hvor han studerer, han kan for en fee caste **Identify** for 15 Gp.

Menny er mere på gulvet og kender nok ikke alle bøgerne i lyet, men ved hvordan man kan finde hver en af dem.

### The Residency
En stor bygning som spreder sig imellem mange træer. Her bor alle de faste beboere i Mylanora. Der findes også diverse andre services her.
