Abreona is a country, in mid-north Redand.

Religion plays a huge part of life in Abreona. The state religion worship [[Tyr, God of Justice]], and [[Torm, God of Courage]]. The church of Abreona controls all parts of the country.

Followers of [[Torm, God of Courage|Torm]] and [[Tyr, God of Justice|Tyr]] believe in Justice, Courage and Self-sacrifice. The judgement of Tyr make sure that everyone is afforded a fair trail. Courage and Self-sacrifice is highly regarded traits of the followers of Torm.
## The King or Queen
The King or Queen of Abreona is largely a ceremonial title inherited by the firstborn child of the king or queen. The title is remains of an older time. Once it held a lot of power, but over the years more and more power went to the church.

Today the king or queen has to swear that they will follow the tenants of Tyr and Torm. [[The High Counsel of Abreona|The high council]] also has to swear fealty to the king or queen. However this oath has been annulled by the Order of Tyr, if the king or queen didn't follow the tenants.

## The Assembly
The assembly of Abreon is composed of 31 clerks. These decide all the laws and policies of Abreona. To enact a law they have to argue why the law follows the word of Tyr or Torm. Then the assembly vote on this.

## The orders of Tyr & Torm
Within the church are the orders of Tyr and Torm.

The order of Tyr sit on the judicial body in Abreona. This means they judge anyone accused of any wrongful action. It is also within this order most Abreonan priests lie. They help the locals decide what to do in hardship.

The order of Torm sit on the executive body in Abreona. Any law enacted by the assembly, they carry out. They are also protectors of the land and it's people. The order of Torm has also been used by the church to build a vast spy network within foreign nations.

## Priesthood
The High Council decide who gets to sit as clerks in the assembly. They also appoint bishop pairs who control the counties of Abreona. In a pair is both a member of the order of Tyr and Torm. Bishops have the power to appoint a number of priests for each city in their county as they see fit.

### Priests of [[Tyr, God of Justice|Tyr]]
A Priest of Tyr spends most of their time praying and studying. This is due to their job mostly consisting of interpreting the word Tyr.

They are required to resolve any disputes within Abreona, or any other place following Abreonian culture. Any judgement made by a member of the order of Tyr, must be made with the Tenents of Tyr and Torm in mind. 

The word and judgement of a priest of Tyr is highly respected. Another job of the Priests of Tyr is to guide any citizen who seek guidens.

### Priests of Torm
The priests of Torm spends their whole life training. They carry out the judgement of Tyr, and they are expected to show the courage of Torm.

Priests of Torm make sure the judgement of Tyr is upheld.

Priests of Torm are also used by the Orders as a special force within the military of Abreona.
