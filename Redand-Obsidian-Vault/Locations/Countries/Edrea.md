Edrea is a country located in the north-west most part of the Redand Coast.
It's only landboarding country is [[Rostral]] to the east.

### Structure
The country is currently run by the [[The Council of Edrea]]. This means that all infrastructure, military forces, and resources in Edrea are technically owned by individual council members, though they are often managed in the name of the council as a whole.

Each council member represents their own noble house, and every family controls a resource vital to Edrea’s prosperity. Officially, these resources are contributed for the good of the nation. In reality, however, resources are frequently traded among the families in a complex web of favors and bargains. While this system is far from perfect, it usually balances out—at least on the surface.
### Culture
For millennia, Edrea has remained isolated, and most of its people have only heard distant tales of the southern wars.

The Eye to the south has made it difficult for any fleet to reach the nation, and a long-standing non-aggression pact with Rostral has further ensured its security.

Most Edreans have long forgotten the atrocities committed by mages in ages past. As a result, magic users are generally tollerated rather than feared—a tolerance likely reinforced by Edrea’s reliance on magic to endure its harsh climate.

Currently, most elves dwell in **[[The Elegant Timberland]]**, as Edreans rarely take kindly to their kind. Their reputation suffers further from the actions of those who live as simple bandits. While having long ears does not make one an outlaw, travelers of elven blood should not expect a warm welcome in the inns.

The most common non-human race in Edrea’s cities is the dwarf. Their resilience and strength make them ideal for the grueling work of mining deep within the **Fertrie** and **Notheim Peaks**, where they extract the ores that fuel Edrea’s industry.

### History
Following the Second Age, the ruler of The Kingdom of Edrea was peacefully deposed by the three largest landowners in the realm. The king had granted the lords too much power, and they believed he no longer had anything to contribute to the kingdom.

However, the rule of the lords was short-lived. When the public learned that the king no longer held any authority, a peasant revolt erupted, demanding to know who truly governed the land. The unrest led to the death of one landowner, while the remaining two fell into bitter disagreement over how to handle the crisis.

Within five months, the region had descended into a full-blown civil war—a brutal conflict that lasted two bloody years.

The war ended when the five most powerful families in Edrea united, pooling their immense wealth and resources to crush all remaining resistance. This was not a difficult task, as these families collectively controlled the majority of Edrea’s military forces.

Each family commanded a vital resource:

- **The lumbermills of [[Arrowhost]]**
- **The iron mines of [[Ironmere]]**
- **The crop fields of [[Maymark]]**
- **The quarry of [[Deepfell]]**
- **The largest mercantile company in [[Windcall]]**

With these resources under their control, the families effectively ruled the entire nation. Recognizing that Edrea would collapse if any one of these resources fell out of government hands, the families agreed to form a [[The Council of Edrea|Council]] to govern collectively. This arrangement has kept the balance of power—and the peace—intact for over a millennium.

Tradition dictates that when a new head rises to lead one of these families, they adopt the family name if they do not already bear it. The name itself carries immense weight and authority. Those few who refused this custom were swiftly overthrown by rivals willing to uphold the legacy.

Thus, to this day, the same five families who ended the civil war 1,354 years ago still hold the seats of power on the [[The Council of Edrea|Council]].
