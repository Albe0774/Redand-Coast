The only tavern in [[Fort Timberpeak]]. Run by the owner by [[Caradoc Miller]], and his son Bardon Miller. It is a quaint little place mainly used as a pub by the soldiers.

There is room for 40 travelers in bunkbeds plus 4 in single/double rooms. All of these are located on the first floor.

On the ground floor there is a pub area, with a larger bar and a smaller kitchen. There are 2 fireplaces, one in the pub area and one in the kitchen.

In the kitchen is a trapdoor leading to a basement filled with meads and ale, plus a small pantry.

Here you can get a drink and a meal. Mainly used by soldiers in their off-hours. Also travelers who long for a solid bed after many days of travel.

A wodden structure. Within there are 2 stone fireplaces. 1 in the main pub and 1 in the kitchen.

The kitchen floor is made with cobblestones. The basement is also made of stone with timbering.
