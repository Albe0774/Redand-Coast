Firstborn and heir to [[Oswaldsön|The Oswaldsön]] estate.
**Brandur** carries the weight of tradition—but he is not content to merely preserve it. Unlike his father Erik, who values diplomacy and restraint, Brandur is driven by a desire to **elevate the family's status** and **reshape their legacy** in [[Edrea]].

### Ambitions

**Political Ascendancy**: Brandur seeks to expand the influence of House [[Oswaldsön]] beyond their estate and into the heart of [[Edrea]].
  
**Economic Expansion**: He envisions turning The [[Oswaldsön]] estate into a hub of trade and craftsmanship, rivaling the Carpenters’ District in [[Arrowhost]]. Brandur has already begun investing in woodcraft, horse breeding, and river trade, hoping to make the estate economically indispensable.

**Restoring Honor**: Brandur is fiercely protective of his family’s name, especially regarding his half-sister Nanna.

**Cultural Legacy**: He has a deep appreciation for history and lore, and plans to expand the family’s holdings in the city’s library and archive. Brandur believes that legacy is not just built through power, but through **preserved knowledge and cultural contribution**.
