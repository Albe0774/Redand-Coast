Davreth is an elf from the Iranapha tribe, from [[The Elegant Timberland]].

A curios elf trying to learn about the human society. Follows the god [[Lara]].

*Core belief:* Life is action and existence. Death is the end. I will do what i can to stay on the god side of the pendulum.  

*Stated belief*: Life and death is natural, and my destiny is to bring others closer to theirs. Liv og død er naturlige stadier, og min skæbne er at hjælpe andre tættere på det stadie de fortjener være på.

*Terror:* Im used to see myself as the arbiter of life and death, therefor i live in ignorance of my own mortality. I have a deep fear of the totality of my existence, and should my goddess fail me, then i probably wouldn't know what to do.

## Appearance
Cream white elf, wearing light wool and linen in earthly colours. Has an barn owl mask, cut out of wood. He has a quite slender build, cream white skin and bald. He has tattoos all over.

## Iranapha
The tribe uses owl masks to signify an individuals role in the tribe.
- *Burrowing owl:* Diplomats or elves who interact a lot with other tribes/elves.
- *Barn owl:* Individuals who meddles in destiny, and manipulates life and death.
- *Great Grey Owl:* Tribal Elders

The tribe also use tattoos to signify family relations and accomplishments. More branches means more close relationships and more leafs means more accomplishments. Face tattoos are reserved for personal accomplishments and body tats are for family accomplishments.
