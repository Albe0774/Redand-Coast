Nyx is a player character, played by Mikkeline. 
Shes a human Barbarian having a background as a chef.
![[Nyx.png|285]]
### Appearance
27 year old, human barbarian. Female. Green eyes. 176 cm height. Long, dark-red, wavey hair. 
### Backstory
Nyx Grew up in [[Snowmire]]. Here she worked as a line cook, and met an on and off again lover ([[Zoya Kanevsky]]).

After having a temper tantrum in the kitchen she got in serious trouble, Zoya helped her getting out of town. They traveled for [[Arrowhost]], on the road Nyx studied under her mentor priest, [[Nadia Perun]]. [[Zoya Kanevsky|Zoya]] pushed her to finding peace and she found it by praying to [[Tyr, God of Justice]].
### Work for the Fallenclaw
She worked for the [[Fallenclaw]] for a few months before helping and subsequently being held prisoner by Kaelen. 

Here she overheard the maids saying that [[Ove Fallenclaw]] is planning to have Gunhild Fallenclaw deposed and take her place. While not knowing exactly who those people are, she knew to keep her head down.

Even though she has been gone from the kitchen in [[Arrowhost]] for some time, she knows she welcome back, as they are missing skilled workers. Tough she might need to explain her absence.
### The Mages
The 3. of Heiliah while Nyx was enjoying a day off she met a group of people on the run. She felt empathy for the group and told them that going south or west would only lead to more people. The only other way to go was north. 

Their leader (Kaelen) decided that Nyx knows to much so she should join them. This Nyx wasn't to keen on and after a scuffle they subdued her.
