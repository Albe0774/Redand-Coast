A human Wizard/Rouge. 15 years of age, brown eyes, black short hair and pale skin. 156 cm tall.
Often seen wearing a cloak, dark rags and wielding a dagger. Has a spell tome with him at all times.

A young kid who believes the authorities should stop interfering in peoples personal lives. Hes quite arrogant and overconfident. Makes very rash decisions.
### Background
Rayn was born 15 years ago on Gavriel the 35. in a cellar under a  residence of a wizard by the name of [[Erhart Hartmann]], in a northwestern quarter of [[Windcall]]. [[Erhart Hartmann|Erhart]] was a kind mand, helping Rayns mother through the birth, however she was weak, after living in the street for a long time. She died in labour, but [[Erhart Hartmann|Erhart]] was able to save Rayn.

[[Erhart Hartmann|Erhart]] chose to adopt the boy, and raise him as his own. [[Erhart Hartmann|Erhart]] was always very careful due to magic users being hunted. Rayn thus had to learn how to be cunning to avoid trouble. 
Through his childhood Rayn helped [[Erhart Hartmann|Erhart]] with his studies and work. This included assistance with magical experiments and magical services to those in need, as well as taking down notes and other assistant work. A lot of work came from a local temple who didn't hesitate to use magic to help the sick. On Rayns 13th birthday he was gifted one of [[Erhart Hartmann|Erhart]]'s old spell books, full of spells for Rayn to one day learn. 

[[Erhart Hartmann|Erhart]] and Rayn seldom stayed at the one place for long, and [[Erhart Hartmann|Erhart]], insisted upon living a modest life to avoid attention. Rayn was however not happy with this, as he thought they deserved a more luxurious life because of their service to the community (and because he always wanted to see how a life with money would look like). In hes early teen years Rayn accidently came in contact with a few of the cities roughians (who without his knowledge were members of the local thieves guild).

One day, when he was 14, Rayn was convinced to steal from a wealthy noble from the [[Machetré|Machetré family]]. This unfortunately led to both him and [[Erhart Hartmann|Erhart]] getting chased out of the city. However the pursuers caught up to them, and [[Erhart Hartmann|Erhart]] tried using a new powerfull, but unproven, teleportation spell to try and save them.

Rayn woke up alone in [[The Elegant Timberland]] without knowing whether or not [[Erhart Hartmann| Erhart]] was still alive. Here he was found by some elves who led him to [[Kelvyn]]. For 11 months, [[Kelvyn]] took care of Rayn and they grew to enjoy each others company.
