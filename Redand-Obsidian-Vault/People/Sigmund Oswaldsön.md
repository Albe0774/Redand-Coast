Sigmund is a human ranger. The youngest of the sons in the [[Oswaldsön]] family.
Curly blond hair. Clean skin, which is barely touched by the sun. Blue eyes. Slim face. Often carrying brigandine, a long-bow and shortsword. He's cloth is generally practical and a bit dirty. 

Most of hes young life he spend in the forest. Later in life he he set out from home going on adventure, this let him to take up a job at [[Fort Timberpeak]].

His brother ([[Brandur Oswaldsön]]) and father would rather he did something more useful for the family legacy, but tolerate his adventures, but only because he's the youngest.
### Mages
Old tradition and upbringing has taught Sigmund to be weary of magic, and other unnatural phenomenon. However his natural curiosity has led him to learn that not all magic is unnatural, and some is especially useful. While traveling in the land of the elves ([[The Elegant Timberland]]) he learned that he had a knack for some magic. Later while resting between adventures Sigmund was contacted by a mage hunter by the name [[Serath Veylan]]. [[Serath Veylan|Serath]] works for [[The Argent Veil]] and wanted Sigmunds help hunting a mage, and as thanks he helped Sigmund focus and control hes abilities with magic.
