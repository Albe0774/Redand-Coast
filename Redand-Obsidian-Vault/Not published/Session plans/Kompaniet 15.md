## Sidste gang
[[Sigmund Oswaldsön|Sigmund]] and [[Clarke Mayhew|Clarke]] decided to return to [[Arrowhost]], while [[Davreth Iranapha|Davreth]] and [[Rayn]] went into the hideout to spy on the Fallenclaw district.

During the day, [[Sigmund Oswaldsön|Sigmund]] talked with [[Serath Veylan]] and got additional information on the evil wizard ([[Kaelen Draith]]) he was hunting, being informed that his quarry might stay in an old, ruined mining village northwest of [[Arrowhost]]. [[Clarke Mayhew|Clarke]] also gathered info that the office of internal affairs has put a 10 G bounty for clearing “squatters” at said ruined village.

During the night, [[Davreth Iranapha|Davreth]] and [[Rayn]] went up into the Fallenclaw district, disguised as a monk and guard, respectively. Skulking around, they heard an unidentified person blackmail [[Hemming Fallenclaw]] into applying pressure on the wood production.

Gathering the day after, the heroes conferred. They decided to go to the ruined village to drive out the “squatters”, find Sigmund’s quarry, and plunder any valuables the squatters may have had, potentially including tools for wizardry and magic. The 10 G bounty will be a nice bonus. To carry potential loot, the heroes rented a mule, and set out north-west.

### Desertøren
[[Kaelen Draith|Kaelen]] og 4 Veil Acolytes (2 med sværd og 2 med crossbows).

*Ruinen er en del af en gammel mineoutpost, fra tiden før kongeriget under herskabet af troldmændene. Det eneste der står tilbage, er en bygning der blev brugt af adelige til administration. Der står flere gamle statuer af dværge som styrede outposten.*

*Outposten blev efter troldmændenes fald, vedligeholdt af kongerne indtil landejerne tog over hvor det blev efterladt. Da det var en mine outpost er der derfor også en sti til en mineskakt bagved outposten.*

[[Kaelen Draith|Kaelen]] er hostile. (Han ved der er nogen efter ham) . Min. 2 acolytes på vagt altid.

Kisten er trapped med en hand crosbow, hvis man åbner den uden at bruge **nøglen** som ligger gemt bagved en mursten ved siden af pejsen.

***Disarm:*** DC 15 Dex (**Sleight of hand/Thieves tool**)**.** On a failed check the trap triggers.
***Dmg:*** DC 13 Con Saving throw. On fail **1d10 + 1d6 poison dmg**, otherwise half dmg.
