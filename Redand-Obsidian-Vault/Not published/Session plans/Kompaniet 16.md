## Sidste gang
[[Kompaniet 15]]
After a day’s journey, the heroes made it to the ruined mining village, finding out that [[Kaelen Draith|Kaelen]] was being protected by a group of minions and that a woman was being held hostage. The heroes decided that [[Clarke Mayhew|Clarke]] should try to lure out [[Kaelen Draith|Kaelen]] and his minions so the rest of the party could ambush them from afar. [[Clarke Mayhew|Clarke]] unsuccessfully tried to intimidate the minions into leaving, leading to an encounter where the heroes killed all but one of the minions. The hostage, a Human Barbarian cook named [[Nyx Ash'kharn]], managed to free herself and aid the heroes, grabbing her trusty meatcleaver that [[Kaelen Draith|Kaelen]] had confiscated.

[[Kaelen Draith|Kaelen]] scolded the heroes for using magical powers to hunt him down for being a wizard. He tried to escape using his powers of teleportation, forcing [[Sigmund Oswaldsön|Sigmund]], [[Davreth Iranapha|Davreth]], and [[Nyx Ash'kharn]] to pursue him. They managed to chase him down and fight him, with [[Nyx Ash'kharn]] dealing the deadly blow. Sigmund retrieved a series of magical artifacts on [[Kaelen Draith|Kaelen's]] body, and [[Nyx Ash'kharn]] helped carry the body back to the village.

While [[Clarke Mayhew|Clarke]] restrained the final minion, [[Rayn]] looted the village, finding some healing potions and an amulet. When the others returned, [[Rayn]] asked [[Sigmund Oswaldsön|Sigmund]] to relinquish some of [[Kaelen Draith|Kaelen's]] artifacts to the rest of the party as payment - however, [[Sigmund Oswaldsön|Sigmund]] refused, wanting to take the artefacts home to [[Serath Veylan|Serath]]. [[Nyx Ash'kharn]] and [[Davreth Iranapha|Davreth]] helped [[Sigmund Oswaldsön|Sigmund]] and [[Clarke Mayhew|Clarke]] bury the bodies of Kaelen and his minions. [[Nyx Ash'kharn]] revealed to the party that she was a cook for one of the wealthy families in [[Arrowhost]], but was kidnapped and has been missing for quite some time. 

Interrogating the final minion, [[Clarke Mayhew|Clarke]] found out that he and the other minions were refugees from [[Windcall]] under [[Kaelen Draith|Kaelen's]] protection. Taking pity on him, [[Clarke Mayhew|Clarke]] decided to release him instead of handing him in to Internal Affairs. [[Rayn]] attuned to the amulet, and [[Davreth Iranapha|Davreth]] received a staff capable of creating fauna. 

The party decided to camp in the ruined village, readying themselves for the coming day.
