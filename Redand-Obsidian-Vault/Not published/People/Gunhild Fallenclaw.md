Gunhild Fallenclaw, is part of the council of [[Fallenclaw]]. She sits as master of coin in the council. She is [[Ivar Fallenclaw]]s most trusted advisor and second in power.

She lives in the Fallenclaw residency in [[Arrowhost]]. 
The same building as Ivar.  

Her private quarters include a small study/office, a living room, a bedroom and a dining room. She shares the westwing of the building with the servants quarters both serving her and [[Ivar Fallenclaw]], however she has the first floor to herself.
