## Ages
In the world of Redand there have been 3 defined ages. The first age has no defined start date, and is therefore measured in BtSA. (Before the Second Age).

The second age lasted 1266 years. And the Third Age has lasted 1684 years so far.
## Months and Seasons
A year last 8 months (2 for each of the 4 seasons (winter, spring, summer and autumn)). The Months are named the following:
- Mailyr
- Aphiel
- Rielat
- Bariel
- Mesha
- Gavriel
- Hieliah
- Azam
Each month last for 39 days (such that a year last 312 days). 
## Days of the week
Each week lasts for 8 days. The days are named:
- Sielday
- Seladay
- Maliday
- Zaladay
- Azraeday
- Alikday
- Suriel
- Gadreul

## Celestial Bodies
There are 2 moons in the sky of Redand.
One smaller called Lacerta, which is grey in the sky. It takes 10 days for Lacerta to complete a cycle, from new moon to another new moon.
The larger is called Zabiel, which is Orange in the sky. And it takes 20 days for Zabiel to complete a cycle, from new moon to another new moon.

This lines up such that every Zabiel new moon also means that Lacerta is a new moon.
