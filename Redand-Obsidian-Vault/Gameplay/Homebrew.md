## Exploration
### Cold/warm conditions
When you buy Clothes *(Fine or travelers)*, choose a range of temp that its acclaimed to. 
For *Fine* this is a range of 5 degrees celsius, and for *travelers* its a range of 10 degrees celsius.

At the end, and start, of every day that a creature predominantly stayed outside of this range, they must make a **Con** saving throw to avoid fatigue. 

The DC is determined by how far above or below the temperature reaches the range set by the creatures clothes.

| **Temp above/below <br>clothe range** | 0-5   | 6-10  | 11-15 | 16-20 | 21-25 |
| :------------------------------------ | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **DC**                                | DC 10 | DC 15 | DC 20 | DC 25 | DC 30 |
*Note:*
- *The rules for a bedroll, and a blanket apply here*
- *Any creature resistant/immune to cold/fire, ignore this condition entirely*

### Downtime while traveling
You can do one down time activity, normally taking one whole day, every other day while traveling slowly. (At *slow speed*)


### Resting
We are playing with Gritty Realism.

A Short Rest is defined as the old long rest (lasting 8 hours with at least 6 hours of sleep *(for elves a total of 4 hours, where they meditate))*

A long Rest is defined as taking a week (8 days) of extended downtime resting *(4 days for elves).*
### Rations
Creating a **ration** of 2 lbs. needs raw meat totalling 4 lbs. of meat.

This takes the better part of a day to do and needs a fire; however you can create more then one ration at a time, and you can do other stuff while the ration is being made.

If you own, and is proficient with **cook’s utensil**, you can create a 2 lbs. **ration** with only 2 lbs. of raw meat.
### Eating
A small or medium sized creature needs to eat 2 lbs. of food each day to ensure no malnutrition (for other creature sizes check PHB under “Malnutrition” (double what it says in the table)).

Eating more than half of the requirement but less than the full amount. The creature needs to succeed a DC 10 constitution saving throw and the end of the day or suffer 1 lvl of exhaustion.

A creature that eats less than half the requirement for 5 days automatically gains 1 Exhaustion level at the end of day.

A creature cannot regain any lvl of exhaustion lost due to malnutrition on days they don’t have a full meal.
### Variant Encumbrance
We use Variant encumbrance listed on p. 176 in the PHB 2014.

Ignore Str. requirement of armour.

You are **encumbered** if you carry weight more than 5 times your str. score. This means your **speed** drops by 10 ft.

You are **heavily encumbered** if you carry weight more then 10 times your str. score. This means your **speed** drop by 20 ft. and you have disadvantage on all D20 tests that use Str. Dex. or Con.
### Training max hit points
You can increase your max hit points up to a max of what you could have rolled.

To gain a hit point you need to train a number of weeks according to the formular: 
$$f(x)=2^x$$
Here *f(x)* is the number of weeks needed to get up to the next level of max hit points.
*x* is how far the max hit point you wanna hit is away from the max hit point you would have been had you taken fixed hit points.

**Example:**
A level 3 ranger rolled for hit points and rolled such that their max is: 20.
If they had taken fixed hit points their max would have been: 22
(note this is without taking into account their con mod).
So in their case: *x=-2*, and:
$$
f(-2)=2^{-2}=0,25
$$
Therefore it would take a quarter week to get to max hit points 21.
## Combat
### Fading Spirit
We use Matthew Mercers *[[The Fading Spirit|“The Fading Spirit”]]* rules for death.:
### Death Saving throws
The DM rolls hidden death saving throws.

A creature can check the status of another unconscious creature by taking the **Study** action, while within touch distance of the unconscious creature. This might require an Intelligence (**Medicine**) check at the DM’s discretion.
### Flanking
Flanking gives +2 to attack rolls rather than advantage.
### Diagonally movement
When moving diagonally on a square grid, every other square cost 10 ft. of movement to enter. Such that the first square cost 5 ft., the second 10 ft. etc.
